The operating model

Grok Bots draft, flag, and brief. Leaders approve, send, publish, and decide. Chat memory dies with the thread — durable decisions live in the knowledge log.

Bots do

  • Draft digests, appeals, posts, and SOPs
  • Flag exceptions, VIP mail, and due-soon risk
  • Brief the morning picture across RCM, ops, and cash
  • Route work through Chief of Staff when asked
Draft Flag Brief Queue

Leaders keep

  • Approvals to send, post, publish, or delete
  • Payer, bank, and vendor contact
  • Prompt / PMS writes and write-offs
  • Roster cuts and new-bot creation

Bot drafts a $1.4k Humana appeal. You say: “Green-light coding review then appeal. Do not email the payer yet.” That one sentence is the whole operating model.

Approval language that sticks

Bots listen for verbs. Use them on purpose.

Green verbs

  • Green-light — proceed on the draft path named
  • Send / publish — human authorizes the external action
  • Paid / hold / skip — stops sticky re-asks
  • Route to X — CoS or specialist owns next step

Standing guards

  • No PHI in owner briefs or knowledge logs
  • RCM fleet is five seats only — never invent extras
  • Overnight never posts remits to Prompt
  • Check Sent before treating mail as still owed
  • Roster recommends cuts; Jordan removes in the sidebar
