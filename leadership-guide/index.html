The operating model

Grok Bots are specialized agents for TriStar work. Chat memory dies with the thread. Durable decisions live in the knowledge log. Leaders should treat bots as a drafting staff with hard lane boundaries.

Bots do

  • Draft digests, appeals, posts, and SOPs
  • Flag exceptions, VIP mail, and due-soon risk
  • Brief the morning picture across RCM, ops, and cash
  • Route work through Chief of Staff when asked

Leaders keep

  • Approvals to send, post, publish, or delete
  • Payer, bank, and vendor contact
  • Prompt / PMS writes and write-offs
  • Roster cuts and new-bot creation

How to ask and approve

Clear language keeps the fleet quiet and useful.

Good asks

  • “Green-light the appeal draft. Hold the write-off.”
  • “Paid invoice #13659 — stop re-flagging.”
  • “Route this to Denial Management. Status only in CoS.”
  • “Draft the posts. Do not publish.”
  • “What changed since 8:30 that needs a yes?”

Standing guards

  • No PHI in owner briefs or knowledge logs
  • RCM fleet is five seats only — never invent extras
  • Overnight never posts remits to Prompt
  • Check Sent before treating mail as still owed
  • Roster recommends cuts; Jordan removes in the sidebar
